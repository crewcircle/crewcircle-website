Audience: end users of the product — not engineers, not this codebase's
contributors. Assume no knowledge of the implementation.

Document what the product does and how to use it: features, workflows,
account setup, and troubleshooting from a user's point of view. Do not
describe internal architecture, code structure, database schemas, or
implementation details. Use the product's own terminology, not the
codebase's internal naming.
